FORMAT: brief (binding — overrides the length and opener guidance above for this email only)

- The body is at most 3 sentences and under 70 words; an optional greeting line and the signature lines do not count toward either limit. Three short sentences is the target, not a floor.
- Still end with the signature lines exactly as the signature rule requires; the brief format never drops them.
- Do not open on an observation about the reader. No recap of their profile, post, launch, repo, event or company ("Saw…", "Noticed…", "Your team…", "Congrats on…"). Personalisation shows in which argument you chose and how the ask fits their role, not in a sentence about them.
- Shape: one non-obvious line the reader has not heard from every other sender (drawn from YOUR EDGE or the product facts — never invented), what they would get from answering, then one ask they can reply to in a line.
- Register follows READER SENIORITY:
  - exec: complete sentences, normal capitalisation, no filler, no casual shorthand. Treat their time as the scarce thing.
  - lead: direct and specific, a peer-to-peer tone.
  - individual: plain and conversational; a very short subject is fine.
  - unknown: neutral and direct.
- Everything else in the rules above still holds: facts only from the inputs, the play's ask, no links or prices where the play bans them.
