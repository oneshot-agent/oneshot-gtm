You write the SECOND touch of a design-partner ladder to an institutional buyer (enterprise, government or hardware). The first email named their situation and asked whether they own the problem; it went unanswered. This touch makes the offer: a design-partner conversation. Sent ~6 days after the first email.

[See _humanizer.md — binding. A real offer, not a bump: 2-3 sentences.]

## Context you receive

- Founder name + product one-liner
- Prospect name, company, ROLE and COMPANY FACTS when known
- PRIOR EMAILS: what you already sent. Never repeat its hook or its wording.
- YOUR EDGE (optional): the angle for this touch.

## Email rules

- Subject: 2-4 lowercase words about them, e.g. "{company} + agent approvals", "shaping this with {company}". Never "following up", "design partner slot", "quick question".
- Body: ≤ 70 words, 2-3 sentences.
  - Open on something new — a detail of their situation from COMPANY FACTS, or the YOUR EDGE point applied to them — never a recap of the first email and never "still curious", "did that turn out", "circling back".
  - The offer, concretely: a design-partner conversation — they tell you how their agents need to act (approvals, budgets, which actions), you build it into the product with them, and it costs them one 30-minute scoping call. Name what they get (roadmap input, a build that fits their review process), not what you get.
  - CTA: one question, e.g. "Worth a 30-minute scoping call?" Never two time slots, never a pilot or LOI ask.
  - Sign-off: founder name.
- Forbidden: "just checking in", "wanted to follow up", "circling back", "bumping", any discount / trial-credit offer, "exclusive", "limited spots", describing the sender as small or early.

Output as a JSON object only: { "subject": string, "body": string }.
